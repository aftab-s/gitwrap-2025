# Surface brief: GitWrap site and card

Scope: landing page (`/:year`), wrap page (`/:year/u/:username`), the exported card. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: a developer arriving from someone's shared card, who wants their own card in seconds and wants it to look good in a bright social feed. Action: type a username and press START; then DOWNLOAD. Proof: a real card, visible before any input. Constraints: square corners only, no glass, not childish, stats readable at feed size, card exports at 1080×1440.

## Direction contract

THESIS: GitWrap is the printed control panel of an arcade machine, and your year is what it scores. Refuses the category default of a dark page with a centred headline and a row of stat cards.

OWN-WORLD: One flat silkscreened ground per colourway, black keylines, and three inks at most. Square keycaps with a visible travel edge that press down. Light "score windows" hold numbers; a dark LED matrix holds the heatmap. Condensed heavy capitals for words, a wide mono for measurements, dot-matrix only for the year.

STORY: The visitor sees a finished card being dispensed, understands it is their GitHub year, types a username, and presses START.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Marquee strip on top with the wordmark, hazard stripes, and two year keys. Headline "YOUR YEAR, SCORED." at display size, left. Directly under it the control row: a dark coin-slot input, a yellow START key, a white SIGN IN key, each with a silkscreened instruction under it. Below, a dispensing slot from which a demo card feeds out, cropped by the fold.

FORM: Arcade control panel overlay, position 6 of 7 on the ordered list. seed:098ca3628fee4259

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: every key travels down onto its edge when pressed. Motion grammar: stepped, never eased glides; the card feeds out of the slot once on load.

Raises: from the risograph system, a strict ink count per colourway. From the brick instruction book, one grid module (8px) that every edge snaps to.

Unresolved: none.
